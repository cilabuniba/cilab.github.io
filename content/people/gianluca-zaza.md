Gianluca Zaza is a researcher at the Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro, and a member of CILab. His research focuses on **Computational Intelligence**, fuzzy systems, Explainable AI, and applications to healthcare.

His work includes neuro-fuzzy modeling, decision-support systems, contactless monitoring of physiological parameters, and interpretable models for cardiovascular risk assessment.
