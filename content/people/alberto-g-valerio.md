Alberto G. Valerio is a Ph.D. Student at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on **Artificial Intelligence for healthcare**, with particular emphasis on medical imaging and Explainable Artificial Intelligence.

His work investigates deep-learning and graph-based methods for analysing biomedical data, including **brain imaging and neurological diseases**, together with methodologies for evaluating and interpreting model explanations.

A further direction concerns the integration of **Large Language Models and structured biomedical information** to support more interpretable and informative AI-assisted analysis.
