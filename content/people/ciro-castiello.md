Ciro Castiello is an Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on **soft computing**, inductive learning mechanisms, interpretability of fuzzy systems, and **Explainable Artificial Intelligence**.

He has participated in several research projects and is a member of the European Society for Fuzzy Logic and Technology (EUSFLAT) and the INdAM National Group for Scientific Computing (GNCS).
