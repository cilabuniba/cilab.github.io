Pasquale De Marinis is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on **Computer Vision and Deep Learning**, with particular attention to learning effectively from limited data and computational resources.

His work includes **few-shot learning and semantic segmentation**, efficient deep-learning architectures and adaptation strategies, and methods for understanding and explaining the behaviour of modern vision models.

A further research direction concerns the deployment of visual intelligence in real-world and resource-constrained settings, including **drone-based and edge AI applications**.
