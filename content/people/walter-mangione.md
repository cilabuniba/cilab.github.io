Walter Mangione is a Ph.D. Student at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on **Artificial Intelligence for the study of brain diseases**, combining graph-based learning with Explainable Artificial Intelligence.

His work investigates **brain connectomes as graph-structured data** and the use of Graph Neural Networks for analysing relationships between brain connectivity patterns and neurological or neurodegenerative conditions.

A central objective is to develop models whose predictions can be interpreted in terms of relevant brain structures and connections, linking **graph learning, medical AI and explainability**.
