Raffaele Scaringi is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research lies at the intersection of **Computer Vision, Deep Learning and structured knowledge representation**.

His work investigates graph-based and knowledge-aware approaches for enriching visual representations with semantic and contextual information, including the integration of **knowledge graphs and visual data**.

A major application area is **cultural heritage and digital humanities**, where these methods support the analysis, organization and interpretation of visual art collections.
