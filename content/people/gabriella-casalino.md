Gabriella Casalino is a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor at CILab, Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro. Her research lies in **Computational Intelligence**, with a focus on intelligent data analysis, eHealth, data stream mining, and **Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)**.

Her work spans image analysis, educational data mining, text mining, bioinformatics, signal processing, and medical applications. She has held visiting research positions at several European institutions and is active in international scientific service.

She serves as **Secretary of EUSFLAT (2025–2027)**, is a Senior Member of IEEE, participates in the IEEE CIS Task Force on Explainable Fuzzy Systems, and serves on editorial boards of international journals. Her research has received recognition including the **FUZZ-IEEE Best Paper Award (2022)**.
