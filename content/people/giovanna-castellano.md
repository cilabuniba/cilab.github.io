Giovanna Castellano is a Full Professor at the Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro, where she coordinates the **Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab)**. Her research interests lie in Computational Intelligence and Computer Vision.

She coordinates the Ph.D. program in Informatics and Mathematics at the University of Bari Aldo Moro and has led or coordinated research activities in national projects, including **NEED — Next-Gen Digital Diagnostics: Bridging AI Explainability and Medical Trust** and the **FAIR — Future Artificial Intelligence Research** project, where she was PI of WP 6.4 on understandability of AI systems.

Her scientific service includes editorial activities, conference organization, peer review, and participation in international and national scientific societies and research networks.

### Current teaching

- Computer Vision, M.Sc. in Computer Science
- Computer Architecture and Operating Systems, B.Sc. programs in Computer Science
