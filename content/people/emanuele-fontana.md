Emanuele Fontana is a Ph.D. Student at the Computational Intelligence Laboratory (CILab), University of Bari Aldo Moro. His research focuses on **trustworthy foundation models and mechanistic interpretability**, with particular emphasis on the real-time detection and mitigation of hallucinations in Large Language Models.

His work investigates **latent-space alignment and shared representations across different model architectures**, with the goal of identifying transferable truthfulness features and developing lightweight detection and steering mechanisms that do not require model-specific retraining, external retrieval systems, or multiple inference passes.

His research also extends these ideas to **Vision-Language Models**, investigating multimodal hallucinations and the alignment between visual and textual representations toward efficient and reusable safety mechanisms for foundation models.
