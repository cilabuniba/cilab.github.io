CILab took part in **ICPR 2026**, held in Lyon, with several scientific and community activities.

We first co-organized the inaugural edition of **PRESTIGE — Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence for Cultural Heritage**, a workshop bringing together research on computer vision, pattern recognition and AI for cultural heritage.

During the conference, **Giovanna Castellano** served as Session Chair for *Scene Analysis and Understanding*. **Pasquale De Marinis** presented two research contributions: *WeedDiffusion* at the ICPR main conference and work from the **VISTA** project at the BRAIN workshop. At PRESTIGE, **Nicola Fanelli** presented research on Multimodal Large Language Models and Token Activation Maps.

During ICPR, **Gennaro Vessio** received an **IAPR Distinction Award**, together with **Guillaume Caron**, in recognition of their strategic leadership of **IAPR Technical Committee 19 on Computer Vision for Cultural Heritage Applications**.
