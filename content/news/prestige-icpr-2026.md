date: 2026-08-21
summary: "CILab took part in ICPR 2026 with research presentations, session chairing and the first PRESTIGE workshop; Gennaro Vessio also received an IAPR Distinction Award."
---

CILab took part in **ICPR 2026**, held in Lyon, with several scientific and community activities.

**Giovanna Castellano** served as Session Chair for *Scene Analysis and Understanding*, while **Pasquale De Marinis** presented two research contributions: *WeedDiffusion* in the ICPR main conference and work from the **VISTA** project at the BRAIN workshop. **Nicola Fanelli** presented research on Multimodal Large Language Models and Token Activation Maps at the **PRESTIGE workshop**.

CILab also co-organized the first edition of **PRESTIGE — Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence for Cultural Heritage**, bringing together research on computer vision, pattern recognition and AI for cultural heritage.

During ICPR, **Gennaro Vessio** received an **IAPR Distinction Award**, together with **Guillaume Caron**, in recognition of their strategic leadership of **IAPR Technical Committee 19 on Computer Vision for Cultural Heritage Applications**.
