CILab successfully organized the first edition of the **PRESTIGE workshop** at ICPR 2026. During the event, Gennaro Vessio received an **IAPR Distinction Award** in recognition of the strategic leadership of IAPR Technical Committee 19 on Computer Vision for Cultural Heritage Applications.
