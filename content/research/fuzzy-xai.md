Explainable AI enables intelligent systems to provide insight into their decisions. Granular Computing offers methodologies for managing imprecision and uncertainty, while Fuzzy Logic provides mathematical tools for developing interpretable models based on graded concepts.

CILab investigates fuzzy and explainable models in several domains, including medical diagnosis, learning analytics and evolving data. The goal is to combine predictive capability with representations and explanations that can be inspected and understood by people.
