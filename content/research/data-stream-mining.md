title: "Data Stream Mining"
order: 3
summary: "Adaptive learning methods for non-stationary data, evolving concepts and continuously changing environments."
---
Many real-world systems generate continuous streams of data whose statistical properties change over time. CILab studies incremental and adaptive learning methods able to update their knowledge as new observations arrive.

Our work includes incremental fuzzy clustering, semi-supervised learning and evolving models for applications where storing or repeatedly retraining on the full data history is impractical.
