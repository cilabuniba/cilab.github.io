Healthcare data are complex, heterogeneous and often affected by uncertainty. CILab develops Computational Intelligence methods for biomedical imaging, physiological signal analysis, digital biomarkers and clinical decision support.

The laboratory has worked on applications ranging from MRI and PET analysis to contactless cardiovascular monitoring, bipolar-disorder assessment and explainable medical AI. A recurring objective is to make models not only accurate, but also robust and understandable in settings where trust matters.
