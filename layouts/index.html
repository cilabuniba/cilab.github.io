Computational Intelligence Laboratory

CILab is a research group of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Bari Aldo Moro, coordinated by Giovanna Castellano.

We work on computer vision, deep learning, fuzzy systems and explainable AI, and apply them to healthcare, agriculture, cultural heritage and road safety.
