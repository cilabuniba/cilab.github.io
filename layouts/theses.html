Theses

B.Sc. and M.Sc. thesis opportunities on CILab research topics, with a strong focus on research practice and reproducible experimentation.

Work on a real research problem.

We offer B.Sc. and M.Sc. theses on our research topics. Occasionally, theses on similar topics can be conducted in collaboration with local companies through external internships. Students may also propose a topic of interest to explore with the group.

Projects primarily use Python and deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch. Outstanding results may contribute to scientific publications or be presented at national or international conferences.

01

Templates

M.Sc. and Ph.D. thesis templates and a research diary are available on Overleaf. A Bachelor thesis template is also available in Italian.

Research workflow

Students are encouraged to document experiments, assumptions, results and open questions throughout the thesis project.

Final presentation

A PowerPoint guide in Italian is available to support the structure of the final dissertation presentation.
